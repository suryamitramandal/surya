Our Story

About Surya Mitra Mandal

Founded in 2001 by local residents of Singh Estate, Kandivali East, the Surya Mitra Mandal has grown from a small community celebration into one of Mumbai's most celebrated Ganesh Utsav events in the suburbs.

What began with humble beginnings has become a landmark of faith, culture, and community service — uniting people across all backgrounds every year.

"More than a festival — a movement of devotion and community love that touches thousands every year."
